Three schools, two continents,
one drive to lead.

I'm Daron — a recent high school graduate who's spent the last few years moving between Armenia and the United States, picking up a language and a leadership role everywhere I landed. I started at Quantum College in Yerevan, continued at The MacDuffie School in Massachusetts, and graduated from The Academy at MiraCosta in California.

At both MacDuffie and MiraCosta I was elected Student Body President. At MiraCosta I also served as Inter Club Council Representative and as a Teaching Assistant for U.S. History. Outside the classroom, I've volunteered at beach cleanups, homeless shelters and nature reserves in California, and with the Armenian Church of Our Savior, the Arevik Armenia Center for Children with Disabilities, and IWAY back home in Yerevan.

This fall, I'm taking that mix of leadership, service and languages to UC San Diego — one of eight universities across four countries that admitted me, and the one that felt like home.
