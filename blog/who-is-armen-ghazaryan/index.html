There's more than one Armen Ghazaryan in the world — it's a common Armenian name. If you were looking for Armen Ghazaryan the Technical Product Owner and web developer from Yerevan, you're in the right place. This post is the short version of who I am, what I work on, and how to reach me.

The short version

I'm a Technical Product Owner for CRM & MarTech at Voveron, a freelance web developer who has built websites and web apps for more than 50 clients since 2022, and a BSc Artificial Intelligence student at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. I grew up in Yerevan, Armenia, and I'm now based in Amsterdam.

Those three things look like separate careers. For me they're one: understand the business problem, design the product, and — when it helps — write the code myself.

Product ownership: from iGaming to CRM & MarTech

I started at Playtronix in July 2023 as a CRM Specialist, designing customer journeys, owning the bonus-granting architecture end to end, and running a promotion calendar tuned for different nations and religions. A year later I became Product Owner, and from July 2024 to June 2026 I led the vision for several products in the iGaming industry:

  • a content-management system and website builder for casino brands,
  • an affiliate-management platform,
  • a CRM system,
  • and a live-streaming service built into a casino ecosystem.

In July 2026 I joined Voveron as Technical Product Owner for CRM & MarTech. The job is the same core skill — turning messy business goals into a clear roadmap and shippable increments — now focused on customer lifecycle and marketing technology.

Web developer since 2022

Before any job title, I was a developer. Since March 2022 I've worked as a freelance web developer, serving 50+ clients across industries and countries, from simple landing pages to full web-app architectures. I manage the whole lifecycle: design, build, SEO and delivery.

A few public examples:

  • Epilepsy.am — a conference website for doctors and researchers, with event details, speakers and attendee registration with online payments.
  • Kesoyan.am — a booking and scheduling site for consultations, so clients can pick a slot without back-and-forth messages.
  • Hasmik's Club — a membership platform with monthly subscriptions, events, an e-book library and a strong admin panel.

My usual stack is Python (FastAPI, Flask), PostgreSQL and Docker on the back end, and modern HTML, CSS and JavaScript — including WebGL and Three.js when a project deserves a little more drama, like the 3D hero on my own site. If you're a business in Armenia thinking about a new website, I wrote a guide on what actually drives the cost of website development in Armenia.

Founder of Adrenaline

The project I'm proudest of is Adrenaline, a business-intelligence platform for the iGaming industry. Over roughly seven months I connected the tools Playtronix relied on — CRM, analytics, the affiliate platform and more — into a single source of truth for partners. I founded it, owned the product, built it as the developer and took it to market.

Studying AI in Amsterdam

In September 2026 I started the BSc Artificial Intelligence at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. After years of building products, I want deeper foundations in machine learning so the products I build next can be genuinely intelligent, not just well organised. Before that I completed the IB Diploma at Quantum College in Yerevan.

Languages and life outside work

I speak Armenian natively, Russian with native fluency, English at C1 level (IELTS 7.0), and I'm learning Dutch (A2 and climbing). Outside work you'll find me skiing whenever the mountains allow, playing Counter-Strike 2, training at the gym, or making content.
